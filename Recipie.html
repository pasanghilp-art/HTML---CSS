How to Make Pancake

Pancake is delicious item and I teach how to make them.

Ingredients

  • flour,
  • oil,
  • milk,
  • egg,
  • baking powder,
  • vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix wet products, i.e. milk, egg, oil, sugar,
  2. Mix dry products, i.e. flour and baking powder,
  3. Mix both and heat the pan,
  4. Put the batter in the pan using big spoon,
  5. After 5 min flip the cake and cook it for another 2 min.
  6. Ready to server
